# LLVM Qualification Group’s August 2026 Sync-up Agenda

Given the summer holiday period, I propose to keep our August meetings lightweight.

This month, let’s briefly recap our current work and short-term priorities, then **open the floor for discussion of new topics and future directions for the WG**. Two timely triggers for this discussion are the acceptance of our talk [LLVM in Safety-Critical Industries: Toward a Shared Qualification Layer Upstream](https://discourse.llvm.org/t/announcing-the-2026-llvm-developers-meeting-program/91470) for the 2026 US LLVM Developers’ Meeting, and the planned discussion on [Lightweight Conformance Test Traceability for LLVM Runtime Libraries](https://discourse.llvm.org/t/rfc-lightweight-conformance-test-traceability-for-libc/91468) at the Runtime Libraries Workshop.

We can also raise any review needs, blockers, or decisions related to open and planned PRs. However, since detailed PR discussions can take considerable time, let’s continue those asynchronously on GitHub and Discord where possible.

As always, other topics are very welcome.
