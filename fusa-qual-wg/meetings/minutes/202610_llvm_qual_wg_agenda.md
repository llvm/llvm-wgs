# LLVM Qualification Group’s October 2026 Sync-up Agenda

Thank you to everyone who has contributed over the past two months, especially to the materials in `llvm-wgs/fusa-qual-wg`, whether through authoring, reviewing or participating in discussions.

As usual, we welcome additional topics and suggestions on priorities from those planning to attend. For this meeting, we propose to focus on **review priorities and feedback on the US LLVM presentation**.

1. **Recent progress and follow-up:** Briefly recap recent merged work and updates on actions from the previous meeting.

2. **Open artifacts and review priorities:** Discuss outstanding questions across the current workstreams. Agree which artifacts would benefit most from focused review and identify any help authors need.

3. **US LLVM presentation feedback:** Consider any comments on clarity, technical accuracy and how the presentation represents the WG’s current work. Agree any changes and a date for further feedback.

4. **Topics from attendees:** Discuss new use cases, concerns or proposed contributions. Identify topics that need a separate discussion or follow-up.

Looking forward to your participation.
