# LLVM Qualification Group’s September 2026 Sync-up Agenda

This month’s sync-ups will be open working sessions, with no dedicated technical topic planned.

Proposed agenda:

1. Review the [open WG pull requests](https://github.com/llvm/llvm-wgs/pulls), focusing on questions, difficulties, decisions needed, and blockers to progress.
2. Discuss any questions or other topics raised by attendees.

Detailed reviews can continue asynchronously on GitHub or Discord where possible, while the meetings can help resolve points that require discussion or coordination.

Please note that I will not be able to attend the EU/Asia-friendly meeting this month, as I will be attending an in-person event. The meeting will be facilitated by another co-host. Depending on work constraints, I may also hand over facilitation of the Americas-friendly meeting. The co-hosting arrangements will be confirmed separately before each call, on Discord.

As always, additional topics are very welcome.
