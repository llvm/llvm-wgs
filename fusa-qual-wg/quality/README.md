# LLVM Quality

This workstream examines LLVM development practices and quality evidence that contributors and downstream users can inspect.
It also explores how to report defects found through Working Group activities and how to understand changes over time.
The [Working Group overview](../README.md#llvm-quality) explains how this work supports more focused qualification activities for tools and libraries.

## Areas of work

| Area | Purpose | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Development-process assessment](process/assessment/README.md) | Observe LLVM's practices and public evidence; compare them with open-source best-practice frameworks; record assumptions, strengths, and evidence gaps; and discuss small upstream improvements of general engineering value. | Ongoing. LLVM's OpenSSF badge entry and a preliminary ELISA assessment provide starting points, but a consolidated Working Group assessment has not yet been published here. |
| [Defect prediction](process/defects-prediction/README.md) | Explore ways to identify code or changes that may deserve closer review, analysis, or testing, including approaches informed by human factors. | Under discussion and planned; methods and outputs remain to be developed. |
| [Defect reports](defects-reports.md) | Consider a lightweight way to connect defects found through Working Group activities to upstream reports and follow their outcomes. | Under discussion and planned; the reporting procedure remains to be developed. |
| [Status reports](status-reports/README.md) | Explore whether periodic summaries of quality-related evidence and changes would help readers follow LLVM over time. | Proposal under discussion; scope, ownership, and frequency have not been agreed. |

The assessment starts with LLVM's [published policies and development-process guidance](https://llvm.org/docs/GettingInvolved.html), together with public records of how the project works.
Open-source frameworks provide questions for examining those practices; they do not define LLVM's development process.
The [assessment page](process/assessment/README.md) describes how evidence from LLVM documents, project activity, and relevant LLVM groups can be compared and made reviewable.

Defect prediction, defect reporting, and any future status reports have separate purposes and are at different stages.
Their linked pages will be developed as those proposals take shape.
